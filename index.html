Tripin

Record what you spent on your trip — and where you spent it.

Get it on the App Store

Key Features

  • Expenses on the map — every expense is pinned where you spent it, so you can remember what you did and where, even after the trip.
  • Photos on every expense — attach multiple photos from your library or the camera, reorder them, and see the cover photo right on the map pin.
  • Trip cover photos — set a photo for each trip so your trip list is easy to scan at a glance.
  • Budget per trip — see total budget, total spent, and remaining budget on one screen.
  • Daily allowance — calculates what you can still spend today from your remaining budget and days left.
  • Automatic currency conversion — expenses in a currency other than your trip's are converted automatically. Past expenses use that day's rate; today's use the latest one.
  • Exchange calculator — enter a local price on the Home screen and see what it costs in your own currency.
  • 20 currencies supported — record expenses in yen, dollars, euros, and more.
  • Spending by category — see food, cafe, transport, lodging, and shopping broken down in Budget.
  • Directions — open a saved expense location directly in Apple Maps.
  • CSV export — save every trip's expenses as a spreadsheet file.
  • No account needed — no sign-up required; everything, including your photos, is stored on your device. Erase everything anytime from Settings.
