Coming to Google Play

Your travel expenses, in one place

  • Budgets for each trip — choose a currency and see your budget, spending and remaining balance.
  • A daily allowance — calculated from your remaining budget and travel dates.
  • Currency calculator — convert local prices on the Home screen. Rates are indicative and may differ from the rate charged by your payment provider.
  • Maps and place search — view expenses with saved coordinates on a map, or record a place name without a location.
  • A category budget — review food, cafe, transport, lodging and shopping expenses.
  • CSV export — save your expense records to a file from Settings.
  • No account required — trip records are stored on your device and may be backed up or transferred according to Android backup settings. Maps, search, exchange rates and ads use external services.
